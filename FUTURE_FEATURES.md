# The Inner Library — Future Features

## 10 Future Feature Ideas

### 1. 🌙 Dark Mode / Evening Theme
A warm, low-light mode for evening use with:
- Deep navy background with cream text
- Reduced blue light tones
- "Goodnight, Library" greeting after 8 PM
- Dimmed bookshelf visuals

### 2. 📅 Weekly Reflection Summary
A gentle weekly recap that shows:
- Patterns in feelings and needs over 7 days
- Most-used tools
- A single "week's sentence" summarizing your journey
- Option to export as a PDF for therapy

### 3. 🔔 Gentle Reminders
Optional, non-intrusive reminders:
- "A page is waiting for you" — once daily, user-chosen time
- "Your Evidence Shelf has space" — after 3 days without entries
- Fully customizable frequency and timing
- Snooze option

### 4. 🎨 Custom Book Covers
Personalize your library:
- Choose colors for each tool's book spine
- Add small icons or symbols to book covers
- Create custom shelf arrangements
- Unlock special covers through consistent use

### 5. 📤 Export & Backup
Data portability features:
- Export all entries as JSON
- Export therapy prep summaries as PDF
- Email entries to therapist directly
- Backup to cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox)

### 6. 🤝 Shared Reflections (Optional)
For those in therapy with a trusted provider:
- Generate a shareable link for specific entries
- Therapist can view (not edit) selected pages
- Password-protected access
- Full control over what is shared

### 7. 🎯 Intention Setting
Add a forward-looking dimension:
- Set a gentle intention for the day/week
- "Today I want to practice..." prompts
- Track intentions without scoring
- Celebrate when intentions are met

### 8. 📚 Guided Journeys
Curated multi-day sequences:
- "7 Days of Boundary Practice"
- "Getting to Know Your Inner Critic"
- "Building Your Evidence Shelf"
- Each journey includes daily prompts and reflections
- Progress shown as a path, not a score

### 9. 🎵 Ambient Soundscape
Optional audio layer:
- Gentle library ambience (page turns, soft rain)
- Breathing prompts for grounding
- Timer for reflection sessions
- Volume control and mute option

### 10. 💬 Quick Capture Widget
Fast entry for moments on the go:
- One-tap "I noticed..." capture
- Voice-to-text for quick thoughts
- Auto-tag based on tool patterns
- Syncs to full library later
